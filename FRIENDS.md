# Lessons learned on parsing particular friends from Drake's Lyrics


## A note on featured artists
A common theme among artists like 21 Savage and Lil Wayne is self-reference in their lyrics. When parsing songs we only want to extract named friends from Drake's part of the song.

# 21 Savage
This artist uses "21" as an adlib when rapping - this does not count.

# Chubbs
"Chubbs" (Nessel Beezer) is Drake's head of security and one of his closest lifelong confidants. He is frequently mentioned.

# Lil Wayne
References to Cash Money / YMCMB are not explicitly references to Lil Wayne; references to the crew can be categorized as their own Friend
